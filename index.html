Sources and limitations

Election data: Pennsylvania Election Returns Report Center, supplied unofficial CSV snapshot. Incumbent/control baseline: PA Senate member list, 2025–26 and PA General Assembly legislative records. The CSV has county × candidate totals, but no precinct denominator or reporting-status field; precinct reporting is therefore shown as unavailable. Treat all leaders and flips as unofficial until certified results are published. Senate baseline mapping in this package covers the 50 current districts; only even-numbered districts are contested in this cycle.
