Sources and limitations

Election data: Pennsylvania Election Returns Report Center, supplied unofficial CSV snapshot. Incumbent/control baseline: PA Senate member list, 2025–26 and PA General Assembly legislative records. Statewide turnout uses Governor votes divided by 9,052,695 registered voters from the official November 3, 2026 General Election registration statistics; the registration total includes active and inactive voters. The CSV has no precinct denominator or reporting-status field. Treat all leaders, flips, and turnout as unofficial/in-progress until certified results and final registration statistics are published. Senate baseline mapping in this package covers the 50 current districts; only even-numbered districts are contested in this cycle.
